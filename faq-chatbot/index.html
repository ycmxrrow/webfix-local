Questions

Answered straight.

What is the FAQ chatbot?

It's a small chat widget for your website. It answers customer questions only from an FAQ list you approve: your prices, hours, area, and policies. It doesn't make things up. Setup starts at $75, with no monthly platform fees.

What kinds of questions can it answer?

The ones your customers ask every day and your list covers: your prices, your hours, the areas you serve, how to book or get a quote, and your policies. You approve every answer before the widget goes live.

What happens when someone asks something not on the list?

It doesn't guess. Questions outside your approved list aren't answered with made-up information — your tap-to-call button and email are right there on the same page for anything the list doesn't cover.

What does it cost after setup?

Setup starts at $75, and there are no monthly platform fees. If your prices or hours change and the answers need updating, that's a content change — order one anytime, or cover it with a Website Care Plan.

Can I try one before I buy?

Yes — the chat bubble at the bottom right of our homepage is one, running live on our own page and answering from our own approved FAQ list. Ask it what a website costs.
